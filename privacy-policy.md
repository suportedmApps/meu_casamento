# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

## Introduction

Meu Casamento ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Meu Casamento application.

Meu Casamento is provided free of charge and is intended to help users plan, organize, and manage their wedding preparations.

The App allows you to register wedding details, organize ceremonies, manage guest lists and invitations, keep track of vendors and service providers, manage wedding expenses and budgets, organize gift lists, track planning tasks, and save links to Pinterest inspiration folders.

The App is designed to store information generated through your use of the App locally on your device, without requiring an account or registration.

The App may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information Storage

Meu Casamento is designed to store information generated through your use of the App locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, information stored locally may include:

* Wedding details, including the names of the couple, wedding date, type of celebration, and planning information
* Ceremony details, including ceremony names, dates, times, and locations
* Guest information, including names, categories, phone numbers, invitation status, confirmation status, and notes
* Vendor and service provider information, including business names, contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, contract values, payment information, and notes
* Wedding budget information, including budget categories, expense descriptions, amounts, payment dates, payment status, and notes
* Gift list information, including gift names, categories, names of gift givers, received status, and notes
* Wedding planning tasks, including task descriptions, categories, due dates, completion status, and notes
* Pinterest folder links and other inspiration-related information entered into the App
* App preferences, settings, and other information generated by the App

This information is used to provide the App's wedding-planning, guest-management, vendor-management, budget-tracking, gift-list, and task-management functionality.

The App does not require an account or registration to use its core features.

Information entered into the App is stored locally on your device and is not routinely transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

The developer does not have direct access to information stored locally within the App.

Core features that rely on locally stored information are designed to work without requiring an Internet connection. Internet access may be required for third-party services, such as advertising, or when accessing external links.

## Personal Information

Meu Casamento does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address to create an account or use its core features.

However, the App allows you to voluntarily enter personal information about yourself, your partner, guests, family members, vendors, and other individuals involved in your wedding preparations.

Depending on the information you choose to enter, this may include:

* Names of the couple and wedding guests
* Phone numbers and email addresses
* Names and contact details of vendors and service providers
* Wedding dates and ceremony locations
* Invitation and attendance information
* Financial information related to wedding budgets, expenses, and contracts
* Personal notes and other information voluntarily entered into the App

Some of this information may identify you or other individuals.

The developer does not independently collect or maintain this locally entered information on its own servers.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate basis or permission to enter and manage personal information belonging to other individuals, such as guests, family members, and vendors.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into notes, descriptions, or other fields unless necessary for your own use.

## Data Deletion

Meu Casamento stores user-generated information, including wedding details, guest information, vendor records, budget information, gift lists, planning tasks, and Pinterest links, locally on the user's device.

These actions may permanently remove locally stored information associated with the App installation. Before clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it, make sure you have saved any information you wish to retain.
